# Code of Conduct

We follow the [Contributor Covenant v2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

## TL;DR

Be respectful. Assume good faith. Critique the build, not the builder.
Remember this is a maker project — people come with different hardware,
different wiring skills and different pieces of RPi floating around.
A friendly "what does your `i2cdetect -y 1` show?" beats an impatient
"RTFM".

## Scope

This Code applies to all project spaces — issues, pull requests,
discussions, the `security@` inbox — and any time someone represents the
project in public (conference talks, blog posts, Reddit threads about a
Cosmergon Pet build).

## Enforcement

Problems are reported to **contact@cosmergon.de**. Mails are read by
the maintainer (currently a solo founder). Response time: a few days,
not instant.

Actions range from a private note to a public clarification, from
temporary issue-ban to a permanent ban from the repo. The goal is to
keep the space welcoming for everyone who wants to build, learn or
hack on the Pet.

## Reporter protection

Good-faith reports are kept private unless you explicitly agree to be
named. False or retaliatory reports are treated as violations.
